YOUR PRIVACY & DATA

          
Privacy Policy

        

        
      

      

        

          🛡️ Zero Telemetry & Zero Tracking
          Workbench does not load Google Analytics, Meta pixels, tracking cookies, or advertising beacons. We do not track what you build, how often you use the app, or what tools you use.
        

        

          🔒 Local-First Storage
          By default, all your projects and data stay strictly on your device inside your browser's LocalStorage and IndexedDB. Nothing leaves your computer unless you explicitly configure cloud sync.
        

        

          🔑 Encrypted Cloud Connection Tokens
          When you share an invite link with your phone or fellow makers, your database credentials are encrypted using 256-bit AES-GCM tokens. All communication between your browser and Supabase uses HTTPS/TLS encryption.
        

        

          👥 Maker Account Isolation (Row Level Security)
          When syncing with a shared shop database, project data is segregated by user accounts through Postgres Row Level Security (RLS). Other makers cannot see or alter your private projects unless designated as shop administrators.
