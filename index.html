THE MAKER JOURNAL

          
About Workbench

        

        
      

      

        
Workbench is an offline-first project journal, shop inventory manager, and craft documentation tool designed specifically for makers, woodworkers, crafters, electronics hobbyists, and artists.

        

          
✨ Core Highlights

          
    
            
  • Offline-First: Works completely without an internet connection. Your projects, materials, checklists, and photos live right in your browser.
    • 
            
  • Private Cloud Sync: Connect seamlessly to your personal Supabase project for multi-device sync and shop collaboration with trusted makers.
    • 
            
  • Skill Progression: Log practice time and earn XP across carpentry, 3D printing, electronics, leathercraft, and more.
    • 
            
  • Workshop Converters: Instant bidirectional fraction-to-decimal calculators, scale sizers, and board-foot estimators.
    • 
          

        

        

          Version 2.4.1 • Free & Open Source • Built for makers everywhere.
        

      

      

        
      

    

    
    
      

        

          
LEGAL & RESPONSIBILITY

          
Terms of Service

        

        
      

      

        

          1. 100% Data Ownership
          You retain full ownership, copyright, and intellectual property rights over all project plans, journal notes, checklists, and photos stored in Workbench. We do not sell or claim rights to your work.
        

        

          2. Self-Hosted Infrastructure
          Workbench runs client-side in your browser and connects directly to your own private Supabase instance. There is no middleman server collecting or intermediating your data.
        

        

          3. Workshop Safety Responsibility
          Physical crafting, woodworking, electrical wiring, and fabrication involve sharp tools, machinery, and dangerous materials. Workbench is purely a record-keeping software tool and does not provide certified engineering or safety advice. Always wear eye protection and follow tool manufacturer safety guidelines.
        

        

          4. Software Provided "As-Is"
          Workbench is provided free of charge without warranty of any kind. We recommend periodically exporting a JSON backup of your projects from the Sync & Backup tab.
        

      

      

        
      

    

    
    
      

        

          
YOUR PRIVACY & DATA

          
Privacy Policy

        

        
      

      

        

          🛡️ Zero Telemetry & Zero Tracking
          Workbench does not load Google Analytics, Meta pixels, tracking cookies, or advertising beacons. We do not track what you build, how often you use the app, or what tools you use.
        

        

          🔒 Local-First Storage
          By default, all your projects and data stay strictly on your device inside your browser's LocalStorage and IndexedDB. Nothing leaves your computer unless you explicitly configure cloud sync.
        

        

          🔑 Encrypted Cloud Connection Tokens
          When you share an invite link with your phone or fellow makers, your database credentials are encrypted using 256-bit AES-GCM tokens. All communication between your browser and Supabase uses HTTPS/TLS encryption.
        

        

          👥 Maker Account Isolation (Row Level Security)
          When syncing with a shared shop database, project data is segregated by user accounts through Postgres Row Level Security (RLS). Other makers cannot see or alter your private projects unless designated as shop administrators.
